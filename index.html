Who is Nakur Wale Baba Ji?

A life devoted to the welfare of others

Shri Shri 108 Swami Ramanand Saraswati Ji Maharaj, lovingly known as Nakur Wale Baba Ji, was a saint whose entire life was devoted to the welfare of others. Born in 1912 in village Bamnauli, he served in the police department for over two decades before beginning his Ramayana discourses — which earned him the title 'Baba' from the department itself. He took sannyas in 1981, and his teaching was simple and universal.

"Man is human first — Hindu or Muslim after."
