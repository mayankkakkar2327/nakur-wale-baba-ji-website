Guru Parampara

About

"Whoever keeps others' welfare in mind, their own welfare follows on its own."

1912

Born in Bamnauli

Born in village Bamnauli, district Baghpat, on Chaitra Shukla Ashtami. His mother Bharti Devi's deep devotion to Shri Ram took root in the child from birth.

Childhood

Jahan Singh — servant of the suffering

Given the name Jahan Singh. Even as a boy, his heart ached for the suffering and was always drawn toward serving others.

1934

Marriage

Married Bhagirathi Devi.

1936

Son born; joined the police

Son Jagpal Singh born. Joined the police department on 18 January 1936.

1940

Steadfast on the path of duty

His wife passed away; he remained steadfast on the path of duty regardless. Served on duty alongside figures such as Gandhiji, Nehruji, Rajendra Prasad and Shastriji.

1949–50

Meeting the guides

In Kanpur, met Shri Gambhiranand Ji and Shri Ramshankar Pathak Ji — the seeds of Ram-devotion sown by his mother fully awakened.

1953–54

Ramayana discourses and the title 'Baba'

Began discourses on the Ramayana at the Saharanpur Police Lines. Even senior officers were moved — and it was the police department itself that gave him the title 'Baba'.

1960

Selfless service, retirement

Continued tireless, selfless discourses through sun and rain, never accepting a single coin for it. Retired from police service in January 1960.

1981

Sannyas — Shri Ramanand Saraswati

Took sannyas (renunciation) from Shri Narayan Muni Ji at Haridwar on 5 September 1981. Moved by his devotion to Ram, his guru gave him the name 'Shri Ramanand Saraswati'.

2003

Mahasamadhi

On the evening of 15 January 2003, at 5:55 pm, he merged into the Divine while absorbed in meditation on Shri Ram. He was given bhoo-samadhi the next day at the satsang hall, Pant Vihar.

Legacy

An imprint that remains

His name and presence remain etched in devotees' hearts to this day — "Blessed are such saints, for the glory of the noble exists only for the good of others" (परोपकाराय सतां विभूतयः).

Succession

Sant Devi Sudiksha Saraswati Ji

His devoted disciple and successor, Shri Shri 108 Sant Devi Sudiksha Saraswati Ji, walks his path today — continuing his work of seva, sacrifice and devotion for the welfare of all.

Sant Devi Sudiksha Saraswati Ji

Sushri Devi Sudiksha Saraswati Ji has been Nakur Wale Baba Ji's disciple since 1969. In 1970 he gave her the Shri Ramcharitmanas as prasad from Haridwar and inspired her to study and reflect on it; in 1971 he placed her in the education department, where her work continues to this day. She has been honoured with the President's Medal for this work, and today continues Baba Ji's path of seva, sacrifice and devotion as his successor.

"Ram te adhik Ram kar dasa, tum te adhik guruhi jiya jaani" — from her own tribute to Baba Ji, reflecting the depth of her devotion to her guru.
